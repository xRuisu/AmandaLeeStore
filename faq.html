Products & Pricing ♡

How much do regular soaps cost?

Our regular handmade soaps are $7 per bar. You can also mix and match any four regular soap bars for $25.

How does the four-for-$25 offer work?

Add any four regular soap bars to your cart, and the bundle discount is automatically applied.

Each complete group of four regular soaps saves you $3 compared with the regular $7-per-bar price. Kids' soaps do not count toward this offer.

How much do kids' soaps cost?

Kids' soaps are $10 per bar. Our listed scents are Baby Powder and Bubblegum.

Are your soaps handmade?

Yes! Amanda Lee Soaps & Bubbles creates handmade soaps in small batches with attention to creativity, quality, and detail.

Custom Requests ♡

Can I request an ingredient substitution?

Yes. On our Shop Soaps page, you can select the ingredient substitution option and describe what you'd like changed.

All substitutions must be reviewed and approved before they can be fulfilled. Not every ingredient can necessarily be replaced.

Does an ingredient request guarantee the change?

No. Substitution requests are not guaranteed. We must confirm whether the requested ingredients and formulation are suitable and available.

Can you guarantee a soap is allergen-free?

We cannot guarantee that a requested substitution makes a soap allergen-free. Please contact us about specific ingredient concerns before ordering.

Sample Packages ♡

Do you offer sample packages?

You can submit a sample package request through our sample selection section .

Sample availability and pricing will be confirmed before ordering.

How many sample scents can I choose?

You can choose up to four different scents in one sample package request.

Each selected scent must be different.

Which sample scents are currently listed?

The currently listed sample options are Cashmere Sweater, Baby Powder, and Bubblegum. Additional scents can be added as they become available.

─── PACKAGED WITH CARE ───

Packaging & Shipping ♡

How are the soap bars packaged?

Every soap bar is individually packaged in a sealed, clear plastic bag.

How will shipping costs be calculated?

Shipping costs depend on the total packed weight of the order, package dimensions, destination, and selected carrier service.

Shipping calculations will include the appropriate packaging materials and box. Accurate carrier rates are not yet connected to the website.

Can I get a shipping price in my cart?

The cart currently accepts a destination ZIP code but does not yet provide a live shipping quote.

A shipping carrier integration is needed before the website can calculate and display accurate shipping charges.

─── SHOPPING MADE SIMPLE ───

Orders & Checkout ♡

Can I place and pay for an order online?

You can browse soaps and add items to your shopping cart.

Online payment processing is not yet connected, so checkout is currently unavailable.

Will my cart save my selections?

Your cart is saved in your browser on the device you're using.

Clearing browser storage or switching devices may remove your saved selections. Your cart does not submit an order to the business.

How can I ask a question before ordering?
